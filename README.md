## 🌟 Overview

**HYPERLINK '98** is a feature-packed desktop operating system and cyberspace portal. In **Layer 2**, HYPERLINK expands backward into the dawn of online culture, allowing users to experience real historical 1980s telecomputing services—CompuServe, Quantum Link (Q-Link C64), The WELL, and Tim Berners-Lee's original CERN World Wide Web—directly inside **Web Explorer**, accompanied by 1200-baud acoustic coupler audio and Commodore 64 SID synthesis.

## 🕹️ Key Features & Applications

### 1. 🌐 Web Explorer '98 & 1980s Telecomputing Archives
- **80s Vintage Quick-Access Toolbar**: Instant one-click jump buttons with retro service badges directly beneath the location bar.
- **1200 Baud CRT Text-Streaming Engine**: Toggle between instant rendering and authentic 1200-baud character-by-character teletype streaming with a one-click "⚡ Turbo" skip button.
- **Historic 1980s Online Services**:
  - `80s-telecomputing.htm`: Central Time Machine hub featuring a 1980-1989 timeline, modem speed benchmark calculator (300 baud vs 1200 baud vs 56k vs gigabit), and trivia.
  - `http://www.compuserve.com/cis`: Authentic CompuServe (1980) recreation with interactive `GO` command prompt, live CB Simulator (Channel 1 public chat with simulated users), UPI news wire, ASCII weather radar, and a live connect charge meter ($6.00/hr).
  - `http://qlink.c64.net/portal`: Quantum Link (1985) Commodore 64 portal (AOL's ancestor) with royal blue C64 styling, People Connection room, and 1541 floppy disk download simulator with real drive seek noises.
  - `http://thewell.org/conferences`: Whole Earth 'Lectronic Link (1985) Sausalito BBS with UNIX PicoSpan prompt (`Ok: `), interactive conferences (Virtual Communities, Cyberpunk & Hackers, Whole Earth, Grateful Dead), and reply posting.
  - `http://info.cern.ch/hypertext/WWW/TheProject.html`: Authentic recreation of Tim Berners-Lee's original World Wide Web page on NeXT with classic green hyperlinks and W3 project architecture.
- **Classic 1990s Web 1.0 Portals**:
  - `welcome.htm`: Flagship portal with visitor hit counter, browser wars poll, webring navigator, and guestbook.
  - `area51/vault99.htm`: Area 51 classified UFO archive with satellite intercepts and alien sighting logs.
  - `napster98/search`: MP3 search engine sharing 56k-optimized chiptunes with a 1-click bridge to WinPulse Player.
  - `altavista98.com`: Superhighway search engine indexing 90s and 80s cyberspace websites.
- **Netscape Bookmarks**: Dropdown bookmarks menu including dedicated 1980s Telecomputing shortcuts.
- **View HTML Source**: Authentic Notepad modal displaying raw source code for all 90s and 80s sites.
- **Modem Audio Triggers**: 56k dialup handshake button + 1200 Baud Bell 212A acoustic coupler sound trigger.

### 2. 💣 Cyber Minesweeper '98
- **Authentic 90s Puzzle**: Beginner (9x9), Intermediate (16x16), and Expert (30x16) grids.
- **Reaction Smiley Face**: Animated face reacting to clicks (`😮`), victories (`😎`), and detonated mines (`😵`).
- **7-Segment LED Displays**: Live remaining mine counter and elapsed game timer.
- **Desktop & Mobile Flagging**: Right-click on desktop or toggle flag mode button on mobile.
- **Record Times**: Automatically records best times in LocalStorage.

### 3. 💬 mIRC '98 (CyberChat IRC)
- **Multi-Channel Chat**: Join `#cyberspace`, `#retro-dev`, `#area51`, or `#warez98`.
- **Active Bot Ecosystem**: Simulated online community (`@SysOp`, `+AcidBurn`, `ZeroCool`, `PixelQueen`, `CrashOverride`, `Morpheus`).
- **Interactive Bot Commands**: `/nick <name>`, `/slap <user>`, `!joke`, `!fortune`, `!calc <math>`, `!quote`.
- **Notification Chimes**: Procedural IRC audio chime on new chat messages.

### 4. 📻 WinPulse '98 Pro (Chiptune Player & Beat Tracker)
- **5 Procedural Chiptune Tracks**: Generates multi-track melodies in real-time (*Cyberspace Drift '98*, *Neon Highway 56k*, *Geocities Under Midnight*, *Silicon Alley BBS*, *Cyberpunk Neon Drive*).
- **16-Step Beat Tracker Sequencer**: Interactive step sequencer grid (Kick, Snare, Hi-Hat, Bass, Lead) allowing users to compose their own 8-bit retro loops.
- **3 Visualizer Modes**: Switch between Segmented LED Bars, Oscilloscope Waveform, and 3D Starfield Warp Tunnel.

### 5. 👾 Cyber Invaders '98 (Arcade Minigame)
- **Playable Space Shooter**: 8-bit canvas arcade shooter with moving alien fleets, laser collisions, and sound effects.
- **Controls**: Arrow Keys / A-D to navigate, Spacebar to shoot (plus on-screen touch controls for mobile).
- **Scoring & Lives**: Score counter, high-score tracking, and 3-heart life system.

### 6. 💻 BBS Cyber-Terminal (MS-DOS Shell)
- **VT100 Green Phosphor Shell**: Interactive command line with prompt (`C:\CYBERSPACE>`).
- **Interactive Commands**: `help`, `dialup`, `matrix`, `sysinfo`, `fortune`, `theme <name>`, `secret`, `clear`.

### 7. 🎨 PixelPaint '98
- **MS Paint-Style Art Studio**: 20-color 90s palette with Pen, Round Brush, Spray Can, and Eraser tools.
- **Adjustable Brush Sizes**: 2px, 4px, 8px, 14px pixel sizes.
- **Export & Clear**: Save your pixel artwork directly as a `.png` file.

### 8. 🗂️ Advanced Window Manager & Desktop UX
- **8-Direction Resizable Windows**: Drag any window border or corner (N, S, E, W, NE, NW, SE, SW) to resize.
- **Double-Click Title Bar**: Quickly toggle maximize/restore.
- **Desktop Right-Click Context Menu**:
  - Display Properties (custom wallpaper patterns: Teal, Cyber Grid, Starfield, Matrix Phosphor, Synthwave Sunset)
  - Cascade Windows & Tile Windows Horizontally
  - Minimize All Windows
  - Quick App Shortcuts

## 🔊 Procedural Audio Engine

All sound effects and music tracks are synthesized on-the-fly using the browser's **Web Audio API**.
- **6 Procedural Chiptune Tracks**: Includes *Cyberspace Drift '98*, *Neon Highway 56k*, *Geocities Under Midnight*, *Silicon Alley BBS*, *Cyberpunk Neon Drive*, and the new **Commodore 64 SID Symphony 1985**.
- **Modem & Telecomputing Synthesis**: 56k V.90 dial-up handshake burst, 1200-baud Bell 212A acoustic coupler FSK carrier chirps, C64 SID arpeggio chords, and 1541 floppy drive head seek motor noise.
- Zero external MP3/WAV assets required — 100% offline and ultra-fast.
- Master sound toggle with mute status indicator in the bottom taskbar.
